## About this entry

The story stays in the most ordinary domestic territory I could find: a neighbour doing a small daily favour, feeding someone's cat while they are on holiday. One detail in that routine is slightly off, the kind of thing you would brush aside as kindness or coincidence. The second sentence never names a threat and never explains anything — it just reports one flat, physical fact about the house, and that fact quietly rewrites every morning that came before it. The fear is left for the reader to finish: whoever made that detail true was there all along, close enough to hide, and has now stopped bothering to hide. The text stops on the word that carries all of that.
